01 · The problem you already know

You've had this conversation. Probably this week.

Someone in your program did everything right. Résumé cleaned up, forty applications out, not one callback. They want to know what they did wrong. The honest answer is usually nothing — the applications died in a tracking system before a person opened one.

  • The hours are the scarce resource. A participant balancing shifts and childcare has maybe six hours a week to job search. Right now most of those hours go into portals that re-type a résumé they already uploaded.
  • The wall is invisible until you've hit it. Nothing on a job posting tells you it runs on a system that will screen you out for a two-month gap, or that it's a twelve-screen application with a knockout question at the end.
  • Coaching this doesn't scale. A caseworker who knows the landscape can steer someone away from the dead ends. That knowledge lives in one person's head, and it walks out when they do.

02 · How it works

Check the wall before you climb it.

It runs before the application, not after the rejection. Thirty seconds, one link, no account for the job seeker.

The output isn't a verdict and it isn't a score to obsess over. It's the thing a good caseworker would have told them if a caseworker had been sitting there: this one's worth your afternoon, this one wants ninety minutes to tell you no, tailor this one first.

Node · ATS detection engine · friction scoring · licensed per organization

03 · Where it actually stands

This is not a finished product, and I'm not going to pretend it is.

I could have written a page that implies you can license this on Monday. You'd find out otherwise on our first call, and then you'd know something about how I do business. So: here's the real state of it.

The detection engine exists and works. The friction scoring exists. What doesn't exist yet is the part only you can tell me: what a program actually needs on the screen, what your reporting requires, what a caseworker would use twice instead of once.

I'm not looking for customers right now. I'm looking for two or three organizations willing to shape it — an hour of your time, honest reactions, and first access when it's real. No cost, no commitment to buy, no contract to sign.
