02 · How to get started

Start here. No jargon required.

JargonToJourney is where the guidance lives: plain-language help, in English or Spanish, for people who have an idea and no map. You don't need to know any technical terms to begin. Translating them is my job.

  1. 01

    Tell me the idea

    Say what you want to make, even if you can't name the parts yet. "An app for my book club" is a real starting point.

  2. 02

    Talk it through

    I offer a free 20-minute intro call, the Trailhead Talk, to hear where you are and what's stopping you.

  3. 03

    Get your map

    Where to start, what you need, which tools fit, and how to work with an AI model, with every term translated.

  4. 04

    Build it

    Build it yourself with that guidance. If you'd rather hand it off, "I Build It For You" is available case by case.

05 · How I work

Forward-deployed. I get to the root of it.

Forward-deployed engineering means the builder sits with the people who have the problem... not three meetings away from them. Sixteen years of community work taught me the real requirements live in the room. So that's where I start.

  1. 01

    Embed

    Show up where the work happens. Listen first, spec second.

  2. 02

    Find the root cause

    Dig under the feature request to the real problem. (It's literally in the name.)

  3. 03

    Build alongside

    Prototype fast with Claude, test on real devices, keep real people in the loop.

  4. 04

    Ship... and stay

    Launch, watch, fix, repeat. Adoption is the finish line, not the release date.

07 · Case study · The one I pulled

The Shielded Path: approved, live... and removed by me.

It was my first app, and Apple approved it. But the protection it promised couldn't be delivered the way it was built, and I wasn't willing to over-promise to the families who trusted it. So I took it off the store myself.

It's being rebuilt on an architecture that can actually keep the promise. Root causes, never shortcuts.

No relaunch date is promised. It returns when it does what it says.

The standard, in practice

SHIPPED
Approved by Apple. Live on the App Store.
PULLED
By me, not by Apple. Removed from sale.
REASON
It couldn't deliver what it promised.
REBUILD
No date promised. It ships when it works.
