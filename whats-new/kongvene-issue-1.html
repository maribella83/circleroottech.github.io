Hi friends,

Welcome to the very first What's New from Kongvene! I'm Maribel, and I build Kongvene for the people who keep a mahjong group going: the texting, the head counts, the "wait, who's bringing the set?"

Tile & ErrorThe stories behind the changes, straight from your feedback.

The endless scroll

One player told me they were tired of scrolling forever to find their next game. Fair! That note is why play days now show up as tiles you can take in at a glance, instead of one long list.

Who is "Player"?

An organizer let me know that some people in their circle were showing up as just "Player." That happens when someone signs up with Apple and keeps their name private. Now Kongvene asks new members for a name when they join, and anyone can change theirs anytime: You tab → Change my name

Bring the sets, plural

A friend who runs more than one table asked how to keep track of sets for a second and third table. Games happen at cafes, restaurants and event spaces, not just around one kitchen table, and Kongvene should keep up. So now every table can track its own set.

The Rack ReportKnown hiccups I'm working on.

On my list

Two sign-ins, two accounts

If you signed up with Google and later sign in with Apple (or the other way around), Kongvene treats those as two separate accounts, so your circle can look like it vanished. It didn't! Sign out and sign back in the same way you first signed up. A real fix that links your sign-ins together is on my list.

Please update!

If you downloaded Kongvene before version 1.5.0, open the App Store or Google Play and tap Update to get everything above. Your circles and play days live in your account, so nothing gets lost when you update.

Thank you for playing, organizing, and telling me when something's off. Please keep it coming: in the app, go to Settings and tap Feedback. I read every note.
