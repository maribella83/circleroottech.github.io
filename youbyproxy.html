01 · The problem you already know

An international move isn't one decision. It's ten.

Moving out of the country means finding a mover you trust with everything you own, a real estate agent who can actually sell before you leave, someone to take what's left, a shipper for what's going with you, a plan for a pet, and — if it applies — an attorney who can tell you what's actually true about your situation. Most people find each of these separately, with no way to know who's trustworthy until it's too late to switch.

  • Every vendor is a fresh search. No shared history, no shared status, no single place that knows the move is even happening.
  • Verification is the client's job right now. Checking a license, a review, a business registration — for every single provider, every single time.
  • Immigration questions get tangled with everything else. People need a referral to someone qualified, not another vendor guessing at legal advice they're not licensed to give.

02 · How it works

One intake. Matched to what your move actually needs.

You describe the move once. YouByProxy matches you to the provider categories that apply — not every category, just yours — and every provider on the platform has already had their identity and license verified before they can be matched to anyone.

The service is free to clients by design: providers pay to be part of the network, not the people moving. A shared status tracker means you're not re-explaining where things stand to six different people — it's in one place, in English or Spanish.

1You submit one intake describing the move — origin, destination, timeline, what you need help with.
2YouByProxy matches you to verified providers across the categories your move actually needs.
3You track every provider's status in one place, instead of six separate email threads.
→Free to you. Providers pay to be part of the network — not the people moving.
